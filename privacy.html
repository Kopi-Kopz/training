Privacy

Training is a personal training tracker used only by its owner.

What data is used

  • Training sets, food entries and body weight that the owner enters in the app.
  • Steps, sleep and resting heart rate read from the owner's own Fitbit account through the Google Health API (read only).

Where it is stored

All data is stored in the owner's own Google Sheet, in the owner's Google account. A copy of recent data is kept on the owner's own devices so the app works offline. No data is stored on this website.

Sharing

Data is not shared with, sold to, or shown to anyone else. Health data from the Google Health API is used only to show the owner's own statistics and training suggestions in the app.

Removing access

Access to Fitbit data can be removed at any time in the Google account under Security → Third-party access.
